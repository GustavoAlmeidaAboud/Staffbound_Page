2.5D Exploration & Side-Scrolling Action

Elemental Staff Combat

Transition seamlessly into side-scrolling combat sections like Mossfall Ravine. Unleash staff combos, gale gusts, entangling roots, teleports, and projectile parries while collecting elemental staves for Fire, Frost, Sun, and Light.

Leaf Parry Recall Blink Leaf Whirl Gale Heartwood Bloom
Alchemy & Shrunk Mazes

Witch Crafting Anywhere

Drop your cauldron anywhere in the world—or relax at camp in The Hollow Within—to brew tonics. Drink a Mouseling Draught to shrink yourself down and slip through secret miniature mazes.

Portable Cauldron Mouseling Draught Polymorph
Living Seeds & Custom Outfits

Mandrakes & Companions

Harvest living seeds outside Old Root's Burrow in Home Glade. They’ll scream when pulled, but you can raise them as companions to help fight enemies—and even customize your companions' outfits!

Living Seeds Battle Companions Custom Outfits
Dynamic Weather & Puzzles

A Living World

Lanterns glow at dusk, seed pods shine at night, and rain and snow roll through the glade. Solve root-and-rune puzzles, and keep your eyes open: the forest keeps its own secrets, and only the curious will find them.

Day / Night / Weather Root & Rune Puzzles Forest Secrets
Hexes & Witch Tricks

Poof! You're a Sheep

Not every fight needs a fight. Cast Polymorph and turn a snarling wolf into something much fluffier. Being a witch is about being clever.
